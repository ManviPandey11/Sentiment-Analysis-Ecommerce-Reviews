Project Title: Sentiment Analysis of Women’s E-Commerce Clothing Reviews
Date: [Insert Date]

Objective:
The objective of this project was to analyze customer reviews of women's clothing from an e-commerce platform to determine the sentiment (positive, neutral, or negative) expressed in the reviews.

Dataset:
Source: Kaggle
Description: The dataset contains 23,486 reviews of women's clothing, including information such as the review text, rating, feedback, and other relevant details.
Steps Followed:
Data Loading and Exploration:

The dataset was loaded into a Jupyter Notebook.
Initial exploration was conducted to understand the structure and content of the dataset.
Key columns such as Review Text, Rating, and Recommended IND were identified.
Data Preprocessing:

The Review Text column was cleaned by removing non-alphabetic characters, converting text to lowercase, and removing stopwords.
Missing values in the Review Text column were handled appropriately.
Sentiment Analysis:

The sentiment of each review was determined using the TextBlob library.
Reviews were classified as positive, neutral, or negative based on the polarity score.
Visualization:

A bar chart was created to visualize the distribution of sentiments in the reviews.
The visualization provided insights into the overall customer sentiment towards the clothing products.
Findings:
The majority of reviews were positive, indicating a high level of customer satisfaction.
Negative and neutral reviews provided valuable feedback for areas of improvement.
Conclusion:
The sentiment analysis provided a clear understanding of customer opinions regarding women’s clothing on the e-commerce platform. The insights gained can help the company improve its product offerings and customer service.

Next Steps:
The project can be extended by building a machine learning model to classify reviews automatically.
Additional visualizations like word clouds could provide further insights into the common themes in positive and negative reviews.
